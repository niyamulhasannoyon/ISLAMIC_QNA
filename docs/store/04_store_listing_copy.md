```markdown
Deen QnA is a minimalist, fast, and authentic digital research archive aggregating indexed Islamic Q&As and scholarly fatwas from verified publications and academic journals.

• Sub-Millisecond Search Engine:
Instant search supporting Bengali, English, and Arabic scripts. Built-in fuzzy matching tolerates transliterations and minor spelling variations.

Every single indexed Q&A clearly attributes the original publishing journal (such as Monthly At-Tahreek, Monthly Al-Kawsar, and Al-I'tisam), the original scholar or mufti, and provides a direct canonical link to the publisher's website.

Filter effortlessly across vital categories:

- Creed & Theology (আকীদাহ ও তাওহীদ)

• Distraction-Free Reader Mode:
Engineered for effortless long-form reading with refined Bengali and Arabic typography, instant permalink copying, and full support for Dark and Light themes.

No registration or account is required. No commercial advertising networks, no user tracking, and no invasive permissions.
```
