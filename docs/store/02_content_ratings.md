# Content Ratings Questionnaires: IARC (Google Play) & Apple App Store

**Application:** Deen QnA (দ্বীন কিউএনএ)  
**Content Domain:** Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh), Scholarly Research & Educational Q&A  
**Classification:** Reference / Educational Archive

---

## 1. Context & Religious Content Sensitivity Guidelines

Deen QnA aggregates and archives published Islamic Q&As from recognized journals (*Monthly At-Tahreek*, *Monthly Al-Kawsar*, *Al-I'tisam*). The content spans classical theological, liturgical, and ethical topics:
- **Daily Worship:** Prayer (সালাত), Fasting (সিয়াম), Charity (যাকাত), Pilgrimage (হজ্জ).
- **Personal Law & Family Ethics:** Marriage (বিবাহ), Divorce (তালাক), Inheritance (ফারায়েজ), Family Relations.
- **Commercial Ethics:** Permissible contracts, Prohibition of Usury (সুদ), Business transactions.
- **Dietary & Lifestyle Guidelines:** Halal and Haram foods, ethical modesty, and prohibitions against intoxicants.

> **Key Reviewer Principle:** All discussions of family law, marital ethics, and ritual purification (*Taharah*) are expressed in formal, classical, academic, and dignified religious terminology. The application contains **no graphic descriptions, no sensationalism, no eroticism, and no promotional depictions of controlled substances**.

## 4. Explanatory Statement for App Store Reviewers (Copy-Paste Ready)

Include the following rationale in the **App Review Information > Notes** field:

```
CONTENT RATING DISCLOSURE FOR APP REVIEW TEAM:
Deen QnA is a digital educational reference library indexing published Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) Q&As from established research journals (Monthly At-Tahreek, Monthly Al-Kawsar, and Al-I'tisam).

The app is rated 12+ solely due to the inclusion of standard Islamic family law and marital jurisprudence (e.g., marriage contracts, divorce arbitration, inheritance shares, and ritual purification). All such topics are treated with the highest degree of academic dignity, decorum, and formal religious phraseology without any graphic, suggestive, or sensationalized depictions.

Similarly, references to alcohol or tobacco occur exclusively in the context of academic legal inquiries examining Islamic dietary prohibitions. The app does not contain unrestricted web browsing, user-generated content, or social networking.
```
